## Interview protocol

1. Build a decision tree and ask one unblocked question per round.
2. Recommend an answer phrased so a simple "yes" accepts it.
3. Record answered questions with their consequences and newly unblocked decisions.
4. Challenge ambiguous terms, conflicting glossary language, and edge cases.
5. Update domain vocabulary as soon as a term is actually agreed; do not mix implementation details into the glossary.
6. Use ADRs only for consequential, non-obvious trade-offs that are costly to reverse.
7. Do not finalize the revised specification or mark the PR ready until the entire decision tree has been reviewed and the owner explicitly confirms shared understanding.
